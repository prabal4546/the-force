Band is on my the easy way to tip is outward, so each arm gets its own distances

Hold your forearm level with your thumb up, like holding a mug: from there the wrist can turn both ways. Twist away from rest to adjust (turning back is free), swipe your forearm sideways, pivoting at the elbow (left or right in the room, however your wrist is turned) to step, tap or double-tap the band.
